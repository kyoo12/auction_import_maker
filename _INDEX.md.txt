# design-inspo — Project Index

## Overview

**What:** A living reference collection of inspiring landing pages and small sites — primarily Mac apps, developer tools, and minimal one-pagers. Curated as we work, to inform future design decisions.
**Why:** Avoid starting from scratch every time we design a page. Build taste vocabulary over time.
**Status:** Active — adding entries as we encounter them. Currently scoped to mdpreview landing page context.

## How to Use This Index

Each entry has:
- **URL** — source
- **Vibe** — 3-word gut feel
- **Palette** — key colors
- **Type** — fonts used
- **What to steal** — the specific thing worth borrowing
- **Tags** — `mac-app` / `cli` / `minimal` / `dark` / `warm` / `monospace` / `one-pager` / `interactive`

## The Collection

### 1. typer.space
**URL:** https://typer.space/
**Vibe:** warm, earthy, approachable
**Palette:** Cream bg `#EBE2D7`, dark brown text `#342618`, soft warm tones throughout
**Type:** SF Pro / ui-rounded / Nunito — system font stack with round feel
**CTA:** "Download for Mac" (dark button) + "Join the waitlist" (outline)
**What to steal:** The warm beige palette is unusually soft for a Mac app — feels premium without being cold. `ui-rounded` font stack gives it personality with zero custom font load. Buttons with `border-radius: 12px` feel modern-native.
**Tags:** `mac-app` `warm` `minimal` `light`

---

### 2. conductor.build
**URL:** https://www.conductor.build/
**Vibe:** stark, developer, no-nonsense
**Palette:** Pure white bg `#FFFFFF`, near-black text `#2C2826`, zero color
**Type:** Geist + SF Mono — full monospace stack
**CTA:** "Download Conductor" (dark) + "Learn how it works" (outline)
**What to steal:** Zero border-radius on everything. Full monospace typography including body copy. Extremely confident — the tool sells itself, the page just gets out of the way. Tailwind-based. Great reference for CLI/developer tool aesthetic.
**Tags:** `mac-app` `cli` `monospace` `minimal` `stark` `dark-adjacent`

---

### 3. onepagelove.com/backups?file=2025-07-29-monotype
**URL:** https://onepagelove.com/backups?file=2025-07-29-monotype
**Vibe:** retro, focused, typewriter
**Palette:** Pure white `#FFFFFF`, black `#000000` — nothing else
**Type:** Times New Roman for everything, 16px flat across all headings and body
**CTA:** None visible — pure product showcase
**What to steal:** Times New Roman used confidently as a *design choice*, not a fallback. Equal font size across all hierarchy levels — the structure comes from spacing and weight, not size. "Retro-inspired digital typewriter designed for flow, focus, and fun." Very tight concept-to-visual alignment.
**Tags:** `one-pager` `minimal` `retro` `light` `serif`

### 4. there.pm
**URL:** https://there.pm/
**Vibe:** clean, friendly, Apple-adjacent
**Palette:** White bg, blue accent `#0070F3` (Apple blue), dark text `#1D1D1F`
**Type:** SF Pro Text + Reddit Mono — system font with monospace accent
**CTA:** Single "Download for macOS" in blue — nothing else
**What to steal:** Maximum restraint: one CTA, one color, nothing competing. Reddit Mono used for secondary code/data display adds just enough technical texture.
**Tags:** `mac-app` `minimal` `light` `single-cta`

### 5. tryklack.com
**URL:** https://tryklack.com/
**Vibe:** playful, warm, satisfying
**Palette:** Warm cream bg `#FFF7ED`, dark stone `#292524`, teal accent `#00BBA7`
**Type:** System sans-serif — ui-sans-serif stack
**h1 size:** 96px — very large, confident
**CTA:** "Mac App Store" (dark pill, shadow) + "FAQs" (light pill)
**What to steal:** The 96px hero headline is bold without being aggressive. `border-radius: 16px` on buttons with a real drop shadow — subtle but adds depth. Warm cream is the best non-white warm background palette in this collection. The teal accent is surprising and fun without ruining the warmth.
**Tags:** `mac-app` `warm` `playful` `one-pager` `light`

### 6. hot-corners.com
**URL:** https://hot-corners.com/
**Vibe:** retro Mac, nostalgic, opinionated
**Palette:** Dark bg `#252022`, yellow `#FCC442`, green `#519553`, red `#ED5056`

### 7. contrasteapp.com
**URL:** https://contrasteapp.com/
**Vibe:** refined, dark, editorial

**Type:** Times New Roman + HelveticaNeue — serif/sans pairing

### 13. celinekeomany.com (video reference)
**URL:** https://x.com/celinekeomany/status/2050458969945055272
**Video:** `/Users/reut/Downloads/Twitter Video Download.mp4`
**Vibe:** editorial, monospace, ghost
**Palette:** Near-white bg, very faint gray text — almost ghosted. Red dot accent (small, used sparingly).
**Type:** Full monospace — lowercase everywhere, no hierarchy through size
**Nav:** Pill-shaped dark floating nav centered at top — `notes / projects / work / chat` — only interactive element visible in the hero
**Texture:** Faint `+` grid pattern across the page — barely visible, adds depth without noise
**Layout:** Centered text block, massive negative space, "explore ↓" as the only CTA (underlined, no button)
**What to steal:** The `+` grid texture is a distinctive touch that adds depth without competing with content. Full lowercase monospace with no size hierarchy — everything reads as equal weight. The pill nav is the dominant visual element despite being small. Extreme restraint: if you removed any one element, the page would lose its character.
**Tags:** `portfolio` `monospace` `minimal` `ghost` `texture` `pill-nav`

## mdpreview Priority Picks — [[2026-05-04]]

For the mdpreview landing page, these 5 are the most relevant:

| Site | Why it's relevant |
|---|---|
| **typer.space** | Same target user (Mac power user), warm palette feels premium without trying too hard, system fonts = zero load |
| **conductor.build** | CLI/developer tool, full monospace, zero decoration — credibility through restraint |
| **onepagelove/monotype** | Times New Roman as a *choice* is bold. If mdpreview leans into the "markdown = writing = text" angle, serif could work |
| **tryklack.com** | Best warm palette in the collection. 96px headline is a good signal for confidence. Pill CTAs with shadow. |
| **celinekeomany (video)** | The `+` grid texture + pill nav + monospace + extreme whitespace is the most distinctive combination. Adaptable to a tool page if the tone is right. |

**Emerging direction for mdpreview:** Warm neutral bg (Klack's `#FFF7ED` or Typer's `#EBE2D7`), full system font or monospace, single CTA, one or two "what to steal" moves from Celine's site (pill nav shape, texture hint). No dark mode needed at launch.
